## 1. Release Overview & Scope

CAS `v4.3.0.0` represents a landmark architectural achievement for the Cycle-Accurate Simulator: achieving **100.0% compliance across all 283 positive simulation tests in the entire IEEE 1800-2023 `sv-tests` standard suite** (spanning Chapters 5 through 26), while maintaining **106/106 (100.0%) milestone regression suites** passing cleanly with zero regressions.

### Key Capabilities Delivered in v4.3.0.0:
- **IEEE Std 1800-2023 §16 SystemVerilog Assertions (SVA) Sequence Operators**:
  - Cycle delay range expressions: `##[min:max]`, `##[*]`, `##[+]`, `##[min:$]`.
  - Binary sequence composition operators: `and` (sequence and), `intersect` (length-matched intersection), `or` (sequence or), `throughout` (condition maintained throughout sequence), and `within` (subsequence containment).
  - Multi-clock sequence step transitions: `##N @(posedge clk)` clock transitions within sequence definitions.
  - SVA sequence match item compound assignments: support for `+=`, `-=`, etc. (`(cond, count += 1)`).
  - Sequence-as-property elaboration and evaluation: automatic wrapping and execution of named sequences used directly in `assert property (seq)` assertions.
- **Sampled Value Functions (§16.9.3)**:
  - Full execution support for `$past(expr [, num_cycles])`, `$rose(expr)`, `$fell(expr)`, `$changed(expr)`, and `$stable(expr)` evaluated against preponed clock history snapshots.
- **100.0% IEEE 1800-2023 `sv-tests` Simulation Suite Parity**:
  - Full pass across all chapters: Chapter 6 (1/1), Chapter 7 (90/90), Chapter 8 (6/6), Chapter 9 (5/5), Chapter 10 (2/2), Chapter 11 (51/51), Chapter 12 (2/2), Chapter 13 (12/12), Chapter 15 (2/2), Chapter 16 (29/29), Chapter 18 (14/14), Chapter 20 (38/38), Chapter 21 (27/27), Chapter 22 (1/1), Chapter 23 (1/1), Chapter 24 (1/1), Chapter 26 (1/1). Total: **283 / 283 (100.0%) PASS**.
