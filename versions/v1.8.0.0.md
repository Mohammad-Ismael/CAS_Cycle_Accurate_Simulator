# Release v1.8.0.0: Procedural Continuous Assignments & Driver Exclusivity

## 1. Overview & Milestone Scope
This release delivers **v1.8.0.0** of the Cycle Accurate Simulator (CAS), implementing full IEEE Std 1800-2023 compliance for:
- **§10.4.2 (Procedural Continuous Assignments: `assign` and `deassign`)**:
  - `assign <variable_lvalue> = <expression>;`: Procedural statement establishing a quasi-continuous assignment overriding regular procedural updates (blocking and non-blocking).
  - Dynamic Operand Sensitivity: Re-evaluates target variables immediately in the Active region whenever any operand in the RHS expression changes.
  - Procedural Write Suppression: All procedural assignments (blocking, non-blocking NBA queues, and delayed NBA updates) targeting an active quasi-continuous net are cleanly suppressed.
  - `deassign <variable_lvalue>;`: Terminates the quasi-continuous assignment, leaving the variable retaining the value it held when deassigned until a subsequent procedural assignment writes to it.
- **§9.2.2.4 (Single-Process Exclusivity & ICPD Check)**:
  - Variables assigned on the LHS of `always_ff`, `always_comb`, or `always_latch` procedures cannot be written to by any other process or procedural `assign`/`deassign`.
  - Rejection with standardized error during elaboration:
    `[CAS-ICPD] Illegal combination of procedural drivers: variable '<name>' written in always_ff/always_comb/always_latch cannot be driven by any other process or procedural assign.`
- **Hierarchical Interconnect & Slice Synchronization**:
  - Full propagation of quasi-continuous overrides and suppression across hierarchical slice bindings (e.g., `testbench.q_out` <-> `dut.q_out`).
- **Standardized Postponed `$monitor` Sampling & Hex Width Formatting**:
  - Strict compliance with §21.2.2: `$monitor` evaluations occur exclusively in the Postponed region of the time slot after all delta cycles settle.
  - Standard `%h` zero-padding to `(width + 3) / 4` hex characters with `%0h` unpadded suppression.

## 2. Key Architecture Achievements & Refactorings

### A. Frontend Lexical & AST Grammar
- **Tokens**: Added `TOKEN_DEASSIGN` (`deassign`) and `TOKEN_ALWAYS_LATCH` (`always_latch`) to `TokenType` in `lexer.hpp` and `lexer.cpp`.
- **AST Nodes**:
  - `ProceduralAssignNode` (`target`, `rhs`): Encapsulates procedural continuous assignment.
  - `ProceduralDeassignNode` (`target`): Encapsulates procedural deassignment.
  - Added flags `is_always_ff`, `is_always_comb`, and `is_always_latch` to `ProcessNode` in `ast_module.hpp`.
- **Parsing**:
  - Extended `Parser::parse_statement()` in `parser_stmt.cpp` to recognize `assign` and `deassign` statements with support for hierarchical dot notation targets (e.g. `dut.q_out`).
  - Marked process types accurately in `parser_stmt.cpp` and `parser_module.cpp`.

### B. Kernel Engine & Event Stratification Integration
- **`Net` Structure**: Added `has_procedural_assign`, `procedural_assign_rhs`, and `procedural_assign_scope` to track quasi-continuous status per net.
- **Hierarchical Interconnect Transitive Synchronization**:
  - Implemented `Evaluator::get_connected_nets()` to trace transitive closure over `slice_bindings`.
  - Synchronizes quasi-continuous assignment and suppression state across bound ports and internal nets without race conditions.
- **Stratified Scheduler (`StratifiedScheduler`)**:
  - In `propagate_comb()`: Evaluates active quasi-continuous expressions on operand changes and updates target nets with width casting.
  - In `commit_nba_updates()` and `transfer_due_delayed_updates()`: Suppresses non-blocking assignment commitments for nets with active quasi-continuous assignments.
  - In `write_slice()`: Suppresses external and continuous slice writes to assigned nets.
- **Postponed `$monitor` Evaluation**:
  - Cleanly separated `$monitor` registration from execution. Evaluation occurs solely in the Postponed region at the end of the time step, ensuring 100% VCS line-for-line parity.

### C. Driver Exclusivity Enforcement (`[CAS-ICPD]`)
- **Elaboration Phase Verification**:
  - In `Elaborator::elaborate()`: Traverses the netlist and AST processes to detect conflicting procedural drivers on variables written within `always_ff`, `always_comb`, or `always_latch` processes.
  - Cleanly catches and rejects illegal combinations of drivers prior to simulation start.
