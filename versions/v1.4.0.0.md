# Release v1.4.0.0: IEEE 1800-2023 §11.4.3 Unary Reduction Operators

## 1. Overview & Milestone Scope
This release delivers **v1.4.0.0** of the Cycle Accurate Simulator (CAS), implementing complete support for **IEEE Std 1800-2023 §11.4.3 (Unary Reduction Operators)** with strict 4-state bit dominance and parity matching against Synopsys VCS.

---

## 2. Key Architecture Achievements & Refactorings

### A. Unary Reduction Algebra (§11.4.3)
- Implemented isolated, pure routines in `LogicOps` (`CAS/include/kernel/logic_ops.hpp`):
  - `reduce_and`: Known `0` dominance over `X`/`Z` states.
  - `reduce_nand`: Bitwise inversion of reduction AND.
  - `reduce_or`: Known `1` dominance over `X`/`Z` states.
  - `reduce_nor`: Bitwise inversion of reduction OR.
  - `reduce_xor`: Strict 4-state unknown propagation; odd parity of known `1` bits produces `1`.
  - `reduce_xnor`: Inversion of reduction XOR (`~^` and `^~`).

### B. Multi-Character Operator Tokenization & AST
- Updated `UnaryOpNode` to store string operators (`std::string op`), handling composite prefix operators (`~&`, `~|`, `~^`, `^~`).
- Extended `Parser::parse_unary()` to disambiguate single-character prefix operators (`&`, `|`, `~`, `^`, `!`, `-`, `+`) from composite two-character reduction prefixes.

### C. Execution Pipeline
- Wired reduction dispatch into `ExprEvaluator::eval()`, running directly against runtime scopes and activation frames without netlist mutations.

---

## 3. Verification & Golden Comparison
- **Benchmark**: `tb_v1_4_0_0_regression` driving `v1_4_0_0_reduction_core`.
- **Golden Reference**: Synopsys VCS 2025.06-SP1.
- **Assertion Result**: `PASS (100% VCS COMPLIANT)` across all 6 reduction operators under known, all-zero, all-one, and mixed 4-state vectors.
